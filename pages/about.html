About RefAI

Empowering African grassroots football with global technology.

Our Mission

RefAI is dedicated to revolutionizing grassroots football in Nigeria and across Africa. We provide local leagues with the exact same tools used by the Premier League: Multi-camera live streaming, VAR Telestration, automated stats, and instant global exposure.

🎯 Vision

To become the standard platform for every community football league, ensuring local talent is recorded, scouted, and celebrated globally.

⚡ Innovation

Leveraging cutting-edge WebRTC, FFmpeg WebAssembly, and low-data transmission protocols to make pro-broadcasting accessible on any smartphone.
